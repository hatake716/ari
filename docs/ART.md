# Visual assets

`app/src/main/assets/woodland-soil.png` was created for ARI using the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-17. It is generated environmental artwork, not a photograph of a real nest. Tunnels, chambers, insects, eggs, obstacles and lighting are drawn dynamically by `NestView`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait Android ant-colony simulator environment background, 1024x1536. Primary request: an extremely realistic orthographic vertical cross section through a temperate woodland floor, photographed like a natural history museum soil monolith. At the top 16 percent: a softly sunlit mossy woodland surface, small blades of grass, a few tiny fallen dry leaves, a thin area of blurred dark forest air. At exactly 18 percent height ground surface begins. Below that, 82 percent of image: continuous solid undisturbed rich brown soil cross-section, fine moist granular textures, clay and layered sediments becoming darker towards bottom, a few delicate fine roots at the upper left and right edges, tiny mineral grains. Even diffuse soft natural light, restrained earthy umber brown, olive green, warm cream highlights. Straight front orthographic camera, no perspective. Entire underground center must be solid soil texture so a game engine can carve and render tunnels on top. No existing burrows, no holes, no tunnels, no ants or other insects, no large stones, no objects crossing the central soil. No text, logo, border, labels or diagrams. Highly tactile photoreal natural materials, detailed but subtle contrast, tranquil, premium nature documentary mood.

No external photographs, textures, music recordings or insect sprites are bundled. Audio is an original synthesized ambient composition generated by the included script, with no sampled recordings.


## Monthly woodland images (1.1.0)

`app/src/main/assets/seasons/month-01.webp` through `month-12.webp` are twelve individually generated seasonal variants of the original environment. They were created with the **built-in ImageGen** tool on 2026-09-17. Each edit preserves the orthographic soil cross section and changes the woodland vegetation, litter, weather and light. No stock photos or third-party assets were imported.

The **exact prompt set**, source output filenames and month-to-asset mapping are recorded in [season-artwork.json](season-artwork.json). Each PNG output was encoded to WebP quality 88 at the original 1024×1536 dimensions, without cropping or resizing, using FFmpeg. The runtime keeps at most two monthly bitmaps in its cache. All twelve images are bundled for offline use.

`AntRenderer.kt` is an independently authored procedural model, with 24 cached walking poses per caste/wing state. It contains jointed legs, a petiole, layered gastral plates, surface grain and setae, compound eyes, toothed mandibles, segmented elbowed antennae, and two pairs of veined wings for alate queens. Antenna scanning uses a separate 16-pose bitmap cache so sensing can continue while legs rest. No AI insect sprite or specimen photograph is used as the ant model.
